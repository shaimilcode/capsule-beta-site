Capsule brings your wardrobe, outfits and plans together. Keep track of the pieces you own, explore combinations, and get optional AI styling help for the days ahead.

Beta help & feedbackHow your data is used

Currently in beta for iPhone. Features may change as testing continues.

01 / Collect

A wardrobe you can see.

Save garments and photos, organise outfits, and keep orders, trips and scents alongside your collection.

02 / Consider

A little styling perspective.

Ask for outfit suggestions using your wardrobe. Optional photo tools can help identify pieces or review a look, with your consent.

03 / Plan

Dress for your day.

Choose calendar events and check the weather for a place to add context to your outfit planning. Calendar connections are read-only.

Your choices shape the experience.

AI, photos, location and calendar features have their own permissions or consent steps. Read the privacy policy for the information each feature shares and the limits of deletion during beta.
