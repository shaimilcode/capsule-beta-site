Privacy policy

This policy describes the public Capsule iPhone beta operated by Shaimil (GitHub: shaimilcode), and this information website. It reflects the data-flow review dated 7 November 2026.

Information you provide

Capsule processes your sign-in information, wardrobe items, outfits, orders, trips, scents, notes and saved conversations. An optional style profile can include your display name, date of birth, height, build, fit and preferences. We also retain consent choices and setup progress.

Supabase handles authentication, including email/password, Apple and Google sign-in. The app stores session credentials in iOS SecureStore. Capsule uses Cloudflare infrastructure for its API, database, private image storage, profiles and calendar snapshots.

Photos

Selected wardrobe photos are resized and compressed before upload to private storage. The retained upload is a normalised copy, not the full-resolution library original. Garment illustrations are rendered on the device. Ordinary AI outfit suggestions do not send your wardrobe photos to OpenAI.

When you choose photo identification, mirror review or screenshot reading, the selected image pixels are sent to OpenAI after the relevant consent step. Screenshot instructions do not redact the image: avoid sending addresses, payment information or other private details. Mirror reviews and conversations may be saved if you choose to save them.

Optional AI processing

AI styling and refinement send your request, relevant wardrobe text, outfit history and, when enabled, style-profile information to OpenAI. Profile use sends calculated age rather than raw date of birth. Authentication email, account ID and display name are not deliberately added to these AI prompts, but your free text can contain personal information.

Styling requires AI consent; photo identification has a separate request-specific consent path. Mirror review and purchase comparison require the applicable styling consent as well. Scent research sends fragrance name and brand to OpenAI and its web-search tool.

Requests are configured with response storage disabled. This does not guarantee zero retention: provider security and abuse-monitoring records may still be retained. Capsule does not promise immediate deletion of information already sent to a provider. See OpenAI’s API data information.

Calendars, places and weather

If you grant permission, selected iPhone calendar events for the next seven days are uploaded, including title, dates, all-day status and location. Optional direct Google Calendar and iCloud connections store encrypted credentials and selected event snapshots. iCloud uses the email and app-specific password you supply. These connections read calendars and do not write events.

Selecting an event for styling can initiate place lookup and weather retrieval. Address queries go to Photon; place searches and forecast coordinates go to Open-Meteo. Foreground location is user-triggered; precise coordinates may be transmitted if your device provides them. Manual place entry is available.

Only the event context selected for an outfit request is sent to OpenAI, rather than an automatic bulk calendar transfer. Meeting links are excluded from that styling context and geocoding, but can remain in the private imported snapshot. Clearing imports does not erase event details already copied into saved outfit notes or styling requests.

Usage records and other providers

Capsule records user-linked AI operation, day, model, status, token counts and provider request IDs. That usage table does not contain prompt or image bodies. Cloudflare may process sampled logs and traces. No dedicated client advertising or marketing analytics SDK was found in the review; Apple, TestFlight, Expo and infrastructure providers may process their own delivery and diagnostic metadata.

Data may be processed in countries where these providers operate. Specific data-residency settings and backup deletion windows were not verified in the review.

Retention and deletion

Your saved account content is kept to provide the app until you remove it or delete your account. Exact retention periods for AI usage records, deletion-status records, place caches, provider records and backups have not all been defined or verified during beta.

Starting account deletion denies access and initiates deletion of the authentication account and owned database/storage records, with retries for incomplete work. Expired calendar authorisation state is cleaned up hourly. Some security, rate-limit and deletion-status records may remain; deletion does not synchronously purge provider records or backups.

Beta deletion limits: account deletion does not currently revoke Apple sign-in authorisation or automatically revoke every Google Calendar grant. Disconnect Google Calendar before deleting your account, and review connected access in your Google account. Revoke an iCloud app-specific password in your Apple account. You can also remove Capsule’s Apple sign-in access in Apple settings.

App-generated temporary image files may remain in device cache until the operating system removes them. Copies you export or share elsewhere remain under your control.

Your choices and requests

You can choose optional features, adjust device permissions, disconnect calendars, remove saved content and request account deletion in the app. The JSON export includes wardrobe records, profile, onboarding, latest suggestions and the current calendar snapshot. It excludes photo binaries and some usage, cache and provider-held records; photos are separate.

For questions about access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights, start through the support page. The public issue tracker is for non-sensitive enquiries only. Do not post passwords, tokens, personal documents or private account details.

This website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no application sign-in, forms, advertising scripts or analytics scripts. GitHub may process connection information to serve and secure the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page as the beta’s features and data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
