Here to help

Capsule support

Found something unexpected, or have an idea for Capsule? Share feedback through the beta’s GitHub issue tracker.

Get help or report a problem

A GitHub account is required to open an issue. Reports are public. Include only a general description, app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. Never post passwords, sign-in codes, calendar contents, personal photos or private account information.

Sending beta feedback

If you installed Capsule through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the Capsule page in the TestFlight app. Check screenshots carefully and remove any personal information before sending.

AI and photo features

Check the relevant consent choice in the app. Photo identification, mirror review and screenshot reading send the chosen image to an AI provider; avoid images containing personal documents, addresses or payment information. AI suggestions may be inaccurate, so review results before saving.

Calendar help

Check phone permissions and selected calendars, then refresh imports. Calendar access is read-only. Clearing imported events removes the snapshot and selections; details previously copied to outfits or styling requests can remain. For iCloud, use an app-specific password rather than your main Apple password.

Account and privacy

Use the app’s account settings for export and account deletion. Before deleting, disconnect Google Calendar and review provider permissions. You must revoke iCloud app-specific passwords in your Apple account. See the privacy policy for beta retention and deletion limits.

For a privacy question, open a general issue asking how to proceed without including identifying account details. Do not use the public tracker to send private data.

Having trouble signing in?

Use the app’s password-reset flow for an email/password account. For Apple or Google sign-in, use the same provider you originally chose. Never share a reset link or one-time code in a support report.
